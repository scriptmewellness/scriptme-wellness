Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to your questions.

If you don't see your question here, send us a message. We'll get back to you within one to two business days.

Who do you treat?

We work with men and women, ages 18 and older. Whether you're managing a chronic condition, looking to optimize your health, or simply want a provider who will actually listen, we'd love to meet you.

Do you accept insurance?

We do not accept insurance at this time. However, your insurance may still cover some of your labs and medications, even when they aren't billed through us. We also accept HSA and FSA payments.

If you have questions about what your specific plan covers, we're happy to help you think through it on a discovery call.

What if I already have lab work from another provider?

Emily is happy to review your existing labs with you. Many patients are told their labs are “normal” even when they don't feel well, because conventional ranges are designed to flag disease, not to optimize wellness.

A 30-minute second-opinion lab review is $50. Emily reviews everything through a functional and optimization lens, looking for patterns that conventional medicine often overlooks.
